Budget

Snytech Solutions is committed to offering cost‑effective IT services without compromising quality. Our pricing structure is designed to minimize expenses by leveraging free and open‑source tools where possible. Domain registration for a .co.za address is available at approximately R150 per year, while hosting through GitHub Pages or Netlify is provided at no cost. Software tools such as Visual Studio Code, GitHub, and design platforms like Figma are used under free tiers, ensuring affordability. Internet and testing costs are estimated at R100, with a small contingency of R50 set aside for unexpected requirements. This brings the total estimated budget for a standard website project to between R300 and R400, making our services accessible to schools, small businesses, and community organizations seeking professional IT solutions.
